Анастасия

Anastasiia Hinak is a FrameUp dancer, instructor, and founder of the FrameUp community in Central Europe, based in Brno and Vienna.

She is known for actively developing the FrameUp scene in Europe, teaching internationally, and inspiring dancers through her unique style and artistic vision.

She is the founder of Frame Nation and FrameUp Austria, a winner of multiple dance competitions, and a recognized performer within the European FrameUp scene.

Anastasiia regularly teaches in Brno and Vienna, leading classes in FrameUp, Strip, and Contemporary.
